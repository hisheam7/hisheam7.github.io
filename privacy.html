Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What the app does with your data

  • Without signing in: your favorites, playlists, history and settings are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us.
  • If you sign in with Google: the app asks for read-only access to your YouTube account (the scope youtube.readonly). It uses this access only to show you your own subscriptions, playlists and liked videos inside the app.

What we collect and store

We do not run any server for the app. We do not collect, store, sell, share or transfer your Google or YouTube data to anyone. The access token that Google gives the app stays on your device and is used only to call the YouTube Data API on your behalf. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SafaTube's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can sign out inside the app at any time. You can also remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device.
