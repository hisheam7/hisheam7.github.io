SafaTube

SafaTube is a simple, distraction-free Android app for watching and listening to YouTube videos: search, favorites, playlists, history, audio-only and background playback.

Sign in with Google (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account to see your own YouTube subscriptions, playlists and liked videos inside the app. The app only asks for read-only access to your YouTube account and never changes anything in it.
